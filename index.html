Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TikTok Automation Tool (the "Application") handles data when you use it to upload video content through the TikTok for Developers API.

1. Information Collection and Access

The Application accesses and processes only the specific data necessary to execute video upload automations on your behalf:

  • Authentication Credentials: The Application utilizes standard secure OAuth access tokens provided by TikTok to authenticate your account. These tokens are stored securely in your local environment.
  • Media Content: The Application accesses locally designated video files, descriptions, and metadata strictly to package and transfer them to TikTok's servers.

2. Data Retention and Storage

The Application does not sell, trade, or share any personal information or media assets with third parties. All communication occurs strictly between your local execution environment and official, secured TikTok API Endpoints.
